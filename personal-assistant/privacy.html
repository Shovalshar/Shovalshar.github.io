Privacy policy

Personal assistant is a private tool used only by its owner, who is also the only user who authorizes it.

  • It accesses Google data (such as Gmail, read-only) only for the account owner who granted access.
  • The data is used only to produce summaries and reminders for that same owner.
  • The data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
